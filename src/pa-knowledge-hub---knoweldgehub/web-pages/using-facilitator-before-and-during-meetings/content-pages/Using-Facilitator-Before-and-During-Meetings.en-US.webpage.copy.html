Using Facilitator Before & During Meetings

Learn when Facilitator can be used, how to enable it before a meeting and how it supports collaboration during live discussions.

Before You Start

Facilitator must be enabled before a meeting begins.

It is available for scheduled Teams meetings but cannot currently be used in instant meetings, Teams calls, channel meetings or unscheduled chats.

Schedule A Teams Meeting

Create a standard Teams meeting and invite attendees.

Enable Facilitator

Turn on Facilitator before the meeting starts.

Start Collaborating

Allow Facilitator to help capture notes and actions throughout the session.

How To Enable Facilitator

When scheduling a Teams meeting and adding attendees, you'll see the option to turn Facilitator on before the meeting starts.

Enable Facilitator when creating a scheduled Teams meeting.

What Facilitator Does During Meetings

01

Capture Notes

Record shared notes during discussions.

02

Track Decisions

Highlight outcomes and agreements reached by the team.

03

Capture Questions

Record open items for future discussion.

04

Track Actions

Identify follow-up tasks and responsibilities.

Using @Facilitator

During the meeting you can interact directly with Facilitator through the Teams meeting chat using @Facilitator.

Notes

Ask Facilitator to capture or summarise discussion points.

Actions

Record tasks and responsibilities as they are discussed.

Agenda

Keep conversations aligned with planned discussion topics.

Key Takeaway

Facilitator must be switched on before your scheduled Teams meeting begins.

Use It Throughout

Capture decisions, actions and meeting notes as discussions happen.

Engage With @Facilitator

Use the meeting chat to interact directly with Facilitator during the session.
