Facilitator Extras & Tips

Facilitator is much more than a note taker. Learn how to get additional value from meetings using prompts, summaries, action tracking and post-meeting review techniques.

Facilitator Can Do More Than Take Notes

Alongside capturing meeting notes, Facilitator can help identify actions, summarise discussions, highlight important decisions and help participants stay aligned throughout a meeting.

Using prompts allows you to get additional insights and organise meeting outcomes more effectively.

Meeting Highlights

Extract important decisions, announcements and contributions from discussions.

Meeting Recaps

Produce structured summaries containing key takeaways and actions.

Action Tracking

Generate lists of actions, owners and responsibilities discussed during the meeting.

After The Meeting

Review Notes

Review the generated notes, actions and decisions to ensure they accurately reflect the discussion.

Confirm Ownership

Check action owners and deadlines before distributing meeting outputs.

Share Outcomes

Share summaries and meeting outputs with colleagues who require visibility of the discussion.

Sharing Facilitator Outputs

Share

To share with other licensed Copilot users, select the Share option from the Facilitator output within Teams.

Copy & Paste

To share with colleagues who do not have a Copilot licence, simply copy the content and paste it where needed.

Always Review

AI-generated content should always be reviewed before being treated as the final record of the meeting.

Things To Remember

01

Shared Content

Facilitator responses are visible within the meeting experience and should be treated as shared meeting content.

02

Review Outputs

Always validate meeting notes, actions and summaries before distributing them.

03

Use Clear Prompts

The more specific your request, the more useful the output is likely to be.
