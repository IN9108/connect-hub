What Is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that can help people answer questions, summarise information, generate content, identify patterns and support everyday work activities.

AI is designed to assist people, not replace them. While it can help improve productivity and reduce repetitive tasks, human judgement, experience and decision-making remain essential.

What AI Can And Cannot Do

AI Can

  • Create draft content and communications
  • Summarise large amounts of information
  • Support learning and knowledge discovery
  • Suggest ideas and recommendations
  • Help automate routine activities

AI Cannot

  • Replace human judgement
  • Guarantee accuracy in every response
  • Understand every business situation perfectly
  • Approve actions on your behalf
  • Override company policies or processes

Terminology You May Come Across

The instructions, questions or requests you give to an AI tool.

The controls and oversight applied to the data used by AI solutions.
