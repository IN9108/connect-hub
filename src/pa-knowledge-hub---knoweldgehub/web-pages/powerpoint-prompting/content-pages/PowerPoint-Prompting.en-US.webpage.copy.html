Creating PowerPoint Presentations With Branding Kits

Learn how to build detailed prompts that help Copilot create professional presentations using approved Interact branding templates.

Why Presentation Prompts Matter

Presentations contain far more structure than a typical document.

A good prompt should tell Copilot who the presentation is for, how many slides are required, what content should appear on each slide and how branding should be applied.

Define The Audience

Tell Copilot exactly who the presentation is intended for.

Specify Slide Count

Explain how many slides should be created.

Describe Each Slide

Give clear guidance on what information should appear throughout the presentation.

What Should A Strong Presentation Prompt Include?

01

Template

Specify the approved Interact branding template to use.

02

Audience

Explain who the presentation is being created for.

03

Slide Structure

Outline the purpose and content of each slide.

04

Design Rules

Include branding and visual guidance.

Example Branding Kit PowerPoint Prompt

Open template from the Interact Internal Branding Kit and create a short, professional PowerPoint presentation titled "How To Use Branding Kits". Keep branding directly from the branding kit and use approved colours, fonts, logos and visual styles. Audience: New employees learning how to create branded content. Presentation Length: 6 slides. Content: Slide 1 - Introduction to Branding Kits. Slide 2 - Why Branding Kits Matter. Slide 3 - Premium Copilot Experience. Slide 4 - Non-Premium Experience. Slide 5 - Best Practice Guidance. Slide 6 - Summary. Design Instructions: • Use approved Interact branding only. • Use approved fonts only. • Keep slides clean and modern. • Use simple visuals and headings. • Do not overcrowd slides. • Maintain accessibility and readability.

Presentation Design Principles

Keep It Simple

Focus on one key message per slide wherever possible.

Use Clear Headings

Help audiences quickly understand the purpose of each section.

Avoid Clutter

Too much content makes slides harder to read and understand.

Branding Rules To Remember

Approved Fonts

Use only fonts included within approved branding guidance.

Approved Colours

Stay within the approved Interact colour palette.

Logo Integrity

Do not alter logo colours, proportions or positioning.

Readability

Ensure content remains accessible and easy to consume.

Common Prompt Mistakes

No Audience

Copilot cannot tailor content if the audience is unclear.

No Slide Guidance

Slide content may become inconsistent and unfocused.

Overly Broad Requests

Generic prompts usually produce generic presentations.

Missing Branding Rules

Important branding standards may not be fully reflected.
