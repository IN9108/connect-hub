Introduction To Facilitator

Learn how Facilitator helps keep Teams meetings organised, focused and action-oriented by capturing notes, decisions, open questions and follow-up actions.

What Is Facilitator?

Facilitator is an AI-powered assistant for Microsoft Teams meetings. It helps teams capture notes, track agenda items, summarise decisions, highlight open questions and record follow-up actions.

Throughout a meeting, Facilitator works alongside participants to help ensure key information is captured and participants leave with a clearer understanding of what was discussed and agreed.

Who Can Use Facilitator?

Facilitator is available to users with a Microsoft 365 Copilot (premium) licence. Once licensed, Facilitator can be enabled within supported Microsoft Teams meetings to assist with note-taking, action tracking and meeting organisation.
