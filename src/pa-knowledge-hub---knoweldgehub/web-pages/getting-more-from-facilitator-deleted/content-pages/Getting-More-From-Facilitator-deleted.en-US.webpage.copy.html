Using Prompts With Facilitator

Facilitator responds to simple prompts entered directly into the Teams meeting chat using @Facilitator.

These prompts can help capture decisions, identify actions, summarise discussions and keep meetings aligned to the agenda.

Summarise Progress

Ask Facilitator to provide an overview of what has been agreed so far.

Capture Questions

Record outstanding questions that still require discussion.

Track Actions

Ensure follow-up tasks are captured as they are identified.
