Need Help With A Policy Or Process?

HR Assistant is available through the People Portal and provides quick guidance on policies, procedures, absence reporting, holidays, IRIS and many other HR-related topics.

Think of it as your first stop for HR guidance before deciding whether you need direct support from the HR team.

Think Of It As Your HR Knowledge Assistant

HR Assistant is a quick-reference knowledge source that helps you understand Interact's internal policies, procedures and ways of working. It gives you fast access to guidance whenever you need it.

What HR Assistant Can And Cannot Do

What It Can Do

Provide guidance on policies, procedures, absence management, employee support, holidays and HR systems.

What It Cannot Do

Raise formal concerns, submit reports or replace direct support from the HR team.

When To Contact HR

You should still contact HR whenever you feel you need direct support or personal assistance.
