Expert Prompt Writing

Expert prompting goes beyond simply providing instructions. It helps Copilot evaluate information, identify risks, challenge assumptions and review its own output before presenting recommendations.

What Is Expert Prompt Writing?

By this stage you will have noticed that prompts are becoming longer and more detailed.

Expert prompting is about helping Copilot understand not only what you want it to produce, but also how it should evaluate information, assess risks, identify gaps and validate recommendations before presenting the final output.

Why Use Expert Prompts?

Stronger Recommendations

Encourage Copilot to explain why actions should be taken rather than simply suggesting them.

Identify Risks

Ask Copilot to consider potential risks, dependencies and unintended consequences.

Higher Quality Outputs

Introduce validation checks before Copilot produces the final result.

The R.O.C.K.E.T.S Framework

Expert prompts build upon the R.O.C.K.S framework by encouraging Copilot to evaluate information, challenge assumptions and review its own work before presenting recommendations.

R

Role

Define who Copilot should act as.

Act as an experienced Operational Reporting Manager.
O

Objective

Clearly define the desired outcome.

Evaluate leadership changes and identify performance impacts.
C

Context

Provide business background and relevant information.

Explain the campaign, objectives and operational environment.
K

Knowledge

Provide supporting materials, reports and documents.

Use all attached reports, policies and supporting performance data.
E

Evaluation

Ask Copilot to evaluate impacts, risks and opportunities.

Consider operational, employee and compliance impacts.
T

Thinking

Encourage strategic thinking and challenge assumptions.

Identify information gaps and alternative explanations.
S

Self Check

Ask Copilot to review its output before presenting it.

Validate recommendations against the objective before finalising.

Expert Prompt Blueprint

Expert prompts provide Copilot with a complete brief and encourage it to think critically before creating the final output.

Prompt Component
Example
What action do you want Copilot to take?
Create an executive-level report for senior leaders in PowerPoint.
How you wish Copilot to act
Act as an experienced Operational Reporting Manager.
Provide links or attachments
Analyse the attached performance reports and supporting data.
Objective
Understand whether the introduction of additional leadership support positively influenced team performance.
Business Context
The campaign contains over 200 colleagues and leadership resource changes are being evaluated.
Audience
Heads of Department, Team Leaders, HR and Senior Leadership.
Input Sources
Use all attached reports, policies and supporting documents.
Before Generating Output
Consider highlights, lowlights, operational impact, employee impact, compliance impact and identify missing information.
Output Requirements
Professional, clear, executive-ready, action-focused and easy to understand using plain English.
Recommendations
Explain benefits, risks and priority levels for each recommendation.
Tone
Professional and concise.
What To Include
Executive Summary, Key Trends, Notable Changes, Risks, Opportunities and Recommended Actions.
Rules & Limitations
Do not simply repeat data. Do not clutter slides. Keep the presentation concise.
Focus
Focus on insights, recommendations and business outcomes.
Design
Use approved branding templates, include speaker notes and follow branding standards.
Final Checks
Is it clear? Is it accurate? Is it actionable? Is it aligned with the objective? Have risks and dependencies been identified?

Before Generating The Final Output

One of the key differences with expert prompting is asking Copilot to evaluate information before producing recommendations.

Highlights

Identify what is working well.

Lowlights

Identify areas of concern or declining performance.

Operational Impact

Explain potential business impacts.

Employee Impact

Consider how recommendations affect colleagues.

Compliance Impact

Highlight any risks relating to policy or compliance.

Information Gaps

Identify information that would improve confidence in the outcome.

Advanced Prompt Vs Expert Prompt

Advanced Prompt

Create an executive-level report and identify key trends within the attached performance data.

Expert Prompt

Before generating recommendations, identify operational, employee and compliance impacts. Highlight any missing information that may affect conclusions, challenge assumptions and validate all recommendations against the stated objective before presenting the final report.
