Automation Basics

Learn how automation can reduce repetitive work, improve consistency and allow more time for higher-value activities.

What Is Automation?

Automation is the use of technology to perform tasks with minimal manual effort. It is commonly used for repetitive, predictable and rules-based processes.

Whilst AI focuses on generating insights and content, automation focuses on completing defined actions and processes.

Save Time

Reduce manual effort for routine tasks and free up time for more valuable activities.

Improve Consistency

Perform tasks in the same way every time to reduce variation and errors.

Increase Efficiency

Complete processes faster and allow teams to focus on customers and outcomes.

Examples Of Automation

Report Automation

Automatically generate and distribute reports on a scheduled basis, reducing manual preparation time and ensuring information is delivered consistently.

Importing Data

Automatically move information between systems without requiring colleagues to manually enter the same data multiple times.

Exporting Data

Automatically extract and distribute information from business systems for reporting, analysis or operational purposes.

When Should Automation Be Used?

  • Tasks performed frequently.
  • Processes with clear rules.
  • Activities that require consistent outcomes.
  • Administrative work that adds little value when completed manually.
  • Processes involving multiple repetitive steps.

When Automation May Not Be Suitable

Complex Decisions

Human judgement is still required for decisions involving risk, interpretation or uncertainty.

Changing Processes

Processes that frequently change may require regular updates and maintenance.

Exception Handling

Some situations require individual review and should not be fully automated.

Automation Opportunity Checklist

  • Do I perform this task regularly?
  • Are the steps usually the same each time?
  • Does the process rely on clear business rules?
  • Could automation reduce manual effort?
  • Would automation improve accuracy or consistency?
