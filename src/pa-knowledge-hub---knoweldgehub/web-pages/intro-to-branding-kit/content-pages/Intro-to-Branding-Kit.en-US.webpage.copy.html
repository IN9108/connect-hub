Understanding Branding Kits

Learn what branding kits are, why they matter and how they help you create professional, consistent content across the Microsoft ecosystem.

What Is A Branding Kit?

Branding kits are managed by Microsoft administrators and provide Copilot with approved company branding assets and instructions.

These kits contain approved templates, colours, fonts, logos and visual standards which Copilot can use when generating content.

Think Of It As A Brand Playbook

A branding kit gives Copilot clear instructions on how Interact content should look and feel, ensuring generated content remains visually consistent and professionally branded.

Templates

PowerPoint, Word, Excel and Designer templates can be included so generated content follows approved company layouts.

Fonts

Branding kits specify approved typography to maintain consistency, readability and accessibility.

Colours

Approved brand colour palettes are made available for use across generated presentations, documents and imagery.

Why Branding Kits Matter

Branding kits deliver three critical business benefits:

Save Time

Spend less time formatting documents, presentations and visual assets manually.

Improve Consistency

Ensure content looks aligned regardless of who creates it.

Professional Output

Use approved branding standards across Microsoft applications.
