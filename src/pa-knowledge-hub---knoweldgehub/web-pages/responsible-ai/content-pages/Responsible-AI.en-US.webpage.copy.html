Responsible AI Usage

AI can be a powerful tool when used correctly. This module explains how to use AI safely, responsibly and in line with business expectations.

Why Responsible AI Matters

AI can help improve productivity, support learning and reduce time spent on repetitive activities. However, AI systems are not always correct and should never replace human judgement.

Every employee remains responsible for the information they create, share and act upon, regardless of whether AI assisted in producing it.

Check Accuracy

AI responses may contain mistakes. Always review outputs and validate important information before using it.

Protect Data

Follow company policies and avoid sharing information that should not be disclosed or processed.

Use Good Judgement

AI can support decisions but should never be treated as the final authority.

Protecting Company Information

When using AI tools, you should always consider what information is being shared and whether you are authorised to use that information.

  • Follow data protection requirements at all times.
  • Do not share sensitive information unless specifically authorised.
  • Only access information appropriate for your role.
  • Ensure business data remains protected and secure.
  • Follow established escalation routes when required.

Understanding AI Limitations

Common Risks

  • Incorrect information
  • Outdated information
  • Missing business context
  • Overly confident responses
  • Misinterpretation of prompts

How To Reduce Risk

  • Review outputs carefully
  • Verify important facts
  • Seek expert guidance when needed
  • Use approved business resources
  • Escalate concerns appropriately

Human Accountability

AI can assist with tasks, recommendations and content generation, but accountability remains with the employee.

Before sharing AI-generated content, ask yourself:

  • Is this accurate?
  • Have I checked the facts?
  • Would I be comfortable attaching my name to this output?
  • Does this comply with business policies?
  • Could this information create risk if incorrect?

Key Takeaways

  • AI should support people, not replace them.
  • Always verify important information.
  • Protect business and customer data.
  • Use approved tools and approved content.
  • Employees remain accountable for decisions and actions.
  • Follow escalation processes when concerns arise.
