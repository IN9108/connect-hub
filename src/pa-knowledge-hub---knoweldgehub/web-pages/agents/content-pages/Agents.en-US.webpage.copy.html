Copilot Bot FAQs

These FAQs cover the most common access and authentication issues users may experience when opening Copilot bots.

01

I Can't See The Bot. What Should I Do?

There are a few common reasons:

  • You may not have been granted access yet.
  • You may be signed in with the wrong Microsoft account.
  • Your access permissions may still be updating.

Try signing out and back in. If the bot still does not appear, contact the bot owner.

02

Why Am I Getting Authentication Pop-Ups?

Some bots connect to Microsoft services such as SharePoint, Teams, Outlook or other approved data sources. These connections may occasionally require you to authenticate.

03

I Keep Getting Asked To Sign In. Is This Normal?

Yes. Microsoft may periodically request authentication to verify your identity and maintain secure access to company data.

04

The Sign-In Window Closed But The Bot Still Isn't Working

Try refreshing the page, reopening Copilot, signing back into Microsoft 365 or clearing your browser cache if using Edge.

Authentication Pop-Up FAQs

When The Window Appears

Select Sign In, use your work Microsoft account, complete any MFA prompts if requested, then return to the bot and continue your conversation.

If It Keeps Appearing

Sign out and back into Microsoft 365. This helps refresh your session and ensures you are using the correct work account.

If It Still Fails

Refresh the page, close and reopen the Copilot app, or clear your browser cache if you are using Edge.
